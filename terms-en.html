1+1=1 — Terms of Use

Updated: 26 September 2026 · English translation; in case of doubt the Slovak version prevails.

1. About the service

1+1=1 is a dating app built on verified real people. By using the app you agree to these terms and to the Privacy Policy.

2. Age

The app is exclusively for people aged 18 and over. You enter your age yourself and we never ask for an ID document.

18+ gate: before anything else the app asks the operating system whether your Apple account belongs to a person aged 18 or over (Declared Age Range interface). Apple tells us only the age range (18–29, 30–44 or 45+) and — if it provides it — whether the age in the account was set by its holder, by a parent in Family Sharing, or verified by Apple (e.g. by payment card or ID) — the date of birth from the Apple account is not transferred. We compare the range with the entered year of birth; a mismatch is reviewed by the operator, not by an automated system. Whoever does not allow age sharing, or whom Apple reports as under 18, cannot enter the app. The same applies to existing accounts the next time the app is opened. 18+ is never decided by age from a photo — only by Apple's gate.

The year of birth you enter in the app is used for the age range and to display your age. If it works out to under 18, the app will not let you continue even if the Apple account says 18 or over. The app requires iOS 26 or later.

We state the limits of this verification plainly: the age in an Apple account is information its holder entered themselves when creating the account (or a parent set in Family Sharing) and no one checked it. Whoever lied there will pass here too.

3. Verification — camera-only photo

During sign-up the app takes your photo directly inside the app — it does not accept a photo from the gallery. A short sequence of frames checks that a live person is in front of the camera and that exactly one face is in the frame.

This check is performed by our own server (api.joinfamiglia.com), not by a third party. The frames are sent to it for face verification and for estimating an approximate age (below); they are not written to disk, and the result kept is a yes/no answer, the degrees of head rotation and the age estimate number.

The frames serve to check that exactly one face is present and that the head really moves. From the frontal frames our server also estimates an approximate age (only the number is stored, never the frame) — solely as a notice for the operator when it differs from the entered year of birth by more than the configured number of years (10 by default). The estimate never decides about access, never blocks anyone automatically and other users do not see it. Access to the app is decided by the 18+ gate (section 2). Gender is not estimated from the face.

Photos in your stash stay exclusively on your phone. When you pick a photo from the stash, it is uploaded to the server as UNPUBLISHED — no one but you sees it and it goes through the content check only once you publish it. If you take a photo with both cameras at once, it includes a small front-camera shot — checked the same way as the main photo; the same applies to the few words and the city with the photo. If you do not decide, the app deletes it after 7 days, the row and the file.

4. Waitlist and entry fee

Signing up and creating a profile are free. When enough verified people gather in your region, the region unlocks — and when at least one of your regions is open (the main one or another one; you count towards the waitlist only in your main region), the app asks for the fee for the first time (currently €5) — it is not a payment for verification (that is always free), it is a filter against non-serious sign-ups.

TIME LIMIT: if a region does not fill up within the set period, IT UNLOCKS ANYWAY — at the number of people reached by then. In that case the fee is NOT REFUNDED. We say this up front so you know what you are getting into: in that case the app starts working with fewer people; it does not mean you paid for nothing.

MERGING REGIONS: if two neighbouring regions are each halfway to the threshold, we may merge them into one larger region and unlock it at once. Your area then expands by the neighbouring region.

FOUNDING MEMBERS (version 1.0): the entry fee has not been launched yet. Whoever finishes sign-up now is a founding member of their main region and has entry FREE FOREVER — the fee will not apply to them even after it is launched. Each region has a set number of founding spots; when they run out, the operator may raise the number or end founding status for new people in the region (sign-up continues). Founding status someone already has is never taken away. A founding code (e.g. from an event or a partner) grants the status even above the number of spots; a code may be limited to a region, a validity period and a number of uses, and the operator may cancel it — whoever redeemed it before keeps the status. Codes are not sold. To prevent guessing, after several wrong attempts the server rejects further attempts for 24 hours.

INVITATIONS AND ORDER: when someone enters your personal invite code, we move you up the line by the number of places the app shows you in advance when inviting. This means users between your old and new place move one place back. We say this openly because it also affects you when someone else invites. No one drops off the waitlist, a place in line can never be bought for money, and redeeming someone else's code gives the person who entered it no advantage — it does not skip the waitlist or the fee.

5. Phone number

The app does NOT ask for a phone number at sign-up. You add it only when needed — when exchanging contacts after a mutually confirmed meeting. It serves EXCLUSIVELY as a contact detail — the app does not verify it and claims nothing about it. It is not proof of identity nor a guarantee of “one account per person”. It is never public, never shown on the profile, and only the person you decide to share contacts with sees it, and only mutually.

6. Location

Precise GPS location is never shown to another user — the app uses it only to assign a region (city), never for anything more precise.

7. Chats and meetings

You can have a limited number of conversations open at once (the current limit is always shown in the app). The app follows the rhythm of a conversation directly on your phone and when it recognises you are ready, it suggests a meeting — the content of your messages is evaluated only on your phone and is sent to the server only ENCRYPTED, in a form the operator cannot read (section 3 of the Privacy Policy). Contact details at a meeting are revealed field by field, always with your explicit consent. When you show interest in someone, a REQUEST is created: you can write to them right away, but the conversation opens — and counts towards the limit for both sides — only once they reply. An unanswered request expires automatically after the set period. The recipient can decline a request without the sender finding out; for the sender the request simply expires. The app does not show whether a message was read. A conversation in which no one writes anything for a longer time (the current period is shown in the app) closes automatically and frees a place in your limit. It is neither a sanction nor the other side's decision — the conversation stays available in History, it just can no longer be written in. A mutually confirmed meeting is never closed this way.

8. Safe Date

Before a meeting you can save a trusted contact (name and phone number) to have at hand. IMPORTANT, AND WE SAY IT PLAINLY: the app itself SENDS NOTHING to this contact and does not track the meeting time. An earlier wording claimed the opposite — it was not true and we corrected it. Safe Date is not and never will be an emergency line and does not contact the police or emergency services. Only you see the contact's name and number and you can delete them at any time.

9. Zero tolerance and moderation

The app has zero tolerance for harassment, hate speech, sexually explicit content and fake profiles. You can report or block another user at any time, from any profile or chat; when reporting you choose a reason from a list and the reported person learns nothing about the report.

When several different people report the same user, or when reports of not showing up to an agreed meeting repeatedly concern them from different people, the app automatically hides their profile — without a human looking at it at that moment. In case of suspicion of a minor this happens at the first report. This is a solely automated decision based on a count (Art. 22 GDPR); the app always tells the affected user directly in the app and offers to request a review by a human. Manual hiding of a profile by an admin is a different case — a human always looked at it. Hiding is not a punishment or a decision of guilt — it is a temporary stop pending review and can be lifted at any time.

Photos are checked before anyone else sees them — when in doubt, the app would rather be stricter.

10. Your account

You can freeze your profile at any time (it disappears from the app, the verification is kept for a return) or delete it permanently directly in the app (Settings → Leave 1+1=1). Deletion requires typing a confirmation word.

11. Liability

The app is provided “as is”. The operator is not responsible for other users' behaviour outside the app or for the course of real meetings — the app offers Safe Date as an aid, not a guarantee of safety. The operator may change or end the service at any time.
